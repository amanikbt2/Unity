Account & Data Deletion Request - Xaylite

At Xaylite, we value your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You have the right to request the deletion of your Xaylite account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Xaylite application on your mobile device.
  2. Go to your Profile Screen.
  3. Tap on Settings.
  4. Scroll down and select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your request to permanently delete your account.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request manual account deletion via email:

  • Send an email to: amanikbt1@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request - Xaylite
  • Include your registered email address or account ID in the body of the email.

Types of Data Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently deleted from our active databases and servers:

  • Your account profile details (Name, Email address, Profile picture).
  • Authentication & Login records.
  • App preferences and personal settings.
  • User messages, chat history, and stored audio data.

Data Retention Period & Processing Time

  • In-app deletion requests are processed immediately.
  • Email deletion requests are verified and processed within 7 business days.
  • No user personal data is retained after deletion, except where required by applicable laws or fraud prevention obligations.
